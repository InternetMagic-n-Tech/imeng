# Backrooms: vision

**What it is.** A single-player liminal horror built on open, shared lore: the Backrooms as an internet legend, with respectful credit to *Escape the Backrooms* (Fancy Games and Blackbird Interactive, published by Secret Mode) and the Backrooms web series. No assets from those works are used.

**Who plays.** One person, alone. They describe their own monster at registration: bigger than a person, their own size, smaller, anything like The Grudge. If they say nothing, it is the tall, thin, lanky one.

**The loop.** Explore levels 0 to 5 and find the seam to go deeper. The monster always pursues; noise brings it closer. The player uses the place against the monster's size (gaps and vents against a big one, stairs and ledges against a small one, hiding and closed doors against an equal). Caught means dead (quietly). Pits injure and disorient, and an injured player is slower. Drowning, and doors that open onto a fall, lava or flooding, then swing shut by themselves when the player is out of sight.

**What it shows off.** Server-owned chase state (`monster_distance`, `injury`) with limits and `exit_when`; judge-filled `monster_size`; accessibility and fear recorded at registration and honoured by the narrator; `/portrait monster` from the registration text with the seed kept; `wrap_up.credits_file`.

**Rules.** Non-graphic. Anything the player says they do not want goes in `avoid` and is honoured at once. Colour is never the only carrier of information. The monster is never shown whole except in a picture the player asked for.

**Done when.** A run ends at the seam (the credits) or at the monster's reach (the credits), each in a few quiet paragraphs, and every hazard was hinted before it could hurt.
